# Cadence

Benchmark: Christian Poensgen, about six posts a week, one every weekday,
264K followers, median roughly 487 reactions and 143 comments per post.

## Shape

- **Six posts a week, written in one half-day batch.** Three from the seed
  theme backlog, two from what actually happened in the codebase that week,
  one refreshed evergreen repost on a three-month rotation.
- **8am ET** (12:00Z, 1pm UK, 2pm DE). The Typefully queue has one slot a day there.
- **Short native text first.** Carousels only once a text post has proven a theme.
- **Commenting is part of the cadence.** Ten outbound comments a day on partner
  posts inside their first hour, plus replies on our own. The hour after
  posting is blocked time.

## Grading

Do not grade a week on its median. Christian's top 10 posts brought roughly
250K of his 264K followers. Review quarterly: did the quarter produce one post
that compounded?

Weekly leading indicator: comment-to-reaction ratio. His headline 1:3 includes
about ten swap-partner comments per post and his own replies; the organic
figure is nearer 1:8, and that is the bar. He advised against paid engagement.

## Platform split

Argue on X; the evidenced version goes on LinkedIn, where the buyers are.
Cross-post only when the copy has been tailored for each.
