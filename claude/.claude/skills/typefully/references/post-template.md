# Post template

```
[HOOK: one line, under 15 words. A thing that happened, or a belief you held
 that turned out wrong. A person, a time, or a number. Never the lesson.]

[TURN: one or two lines. What you found out, or what it cost. The gap between
 what you expected and what happened.]

[CLAIM: one line. The principle, stated so someone could disagree with it.]

[HOW: 3 to 5 short lines. What you actually do now. Concrete actions a reader
 could do this afternoon, not advice.]

[CLOSE: one line. A question that invites a reply, ideally echoing a phrase
 from earlier in the post, or the claim restated harder.]
```

## Constraints

- One idea. If a second one appears, it is the next post.
- Nothing before the hook. No "I've been thinking about", no emoji, no thread marker.
- No link in the body. The link goes in the first comment.
- 120 to 180 words. Short lines, blank line between blocks.
- LinkedIn shows about 140 characters, two lines on mobile, before "...more".
  The hook must land its reversal inside that.
- LinkedIn has no markdown. Bullet options: `-`, `•`, `→`, or `1.` numbers.
  Pick one style per post and keep punctuation consistent across lines.
- "Measure", not "visualise". Counts, not percentages, when the base is small.
- A named customer, a number, or Brendan's own mistake makes it personal.
  Low stakes topic, high quality writing: the topic can be small, the craft cannot.

## Ship checklist

A draft ships when all of these hold:

- Hook is one line, under 15 words, no preamble
- Hook reverses a belief the reader holds, or opens on a named person
- Exactly one idea in the post
- Something in it is arguable: a reader who disagrees knows where to push
- No link in the body
- Native text or a carousel, never a link share
- Personal: it happened to Brendan, to a Sourcery customer, or it is our data
- Every figure in the post came from a corpus or a ticket we can point to

## After posting

Log the publish time. At one hour, check the comment:reaction shape. Organic
target is nearer 1:8 than the engineered 1:3. 200 reactions and 6 comments is
the wrong shape; 200 and 25 is on track. Reply to every comment in that hour.
